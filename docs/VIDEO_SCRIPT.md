# Demo video script (about 2½ minutes)

Record on a phone, or in the browser's mobile view at 390px wide. Click **Reset demo data** in the GoodCane team view first so the numbers match.

| Time | Screen | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home | "GoodCane presses fresh juice once a week. People find it on TikTok, but interest doesn't become a routine, and GoodCane can't see demand before production day. This prototype fixes that with weekly pre-orders and one-tap reorders." (Open the link with `?src=tiktok`.) |
| 0:15 | Home → Pre-order | Show the next batch card and the countdown to the cutoff. Tap **Pre-order now**. |
| 0:25 | Order form | Add 2 × Mint & Lemon, 1 × Tigernut Coconut. Choose Pickup point A. Enter name and number. **Tick the reminder opt-in** ("off by default, at most weekly"). Note "No payment now". Send. |
| 0:50 | Receipt | Point at the **Requested** status: "Not confirmed or paid yet." Mention the customer can cancel until Thursday 18:00. |
| 1:00 | GoodCane team → Demand | "Confirmed bottles vs requests awaiting confirmation, per flavour. The suggested batch weights pending requests by how often they've been confirmed before." |
| 1:15 | Orders | Filter **Requested**. **Confirm** the new order, then **Mark paid** (payment happens outside the app). Back to Demand: the confirmed number goes up. |
| 1:30 | Reminders | "Customers who opted in and haven't ordered yet. Sending is switched off until GoodCane approves it." Show the message preview with the **Same again?** link. |
| 1:45 | Customer → My orders | "A week later the customer comes back." Show the order history with the **PAID** status. Tap **Same again**: it's prefilled for *next* week. Send. Two orders, two weeks. |
| 2:05 | Results | Repeat-order rate, confirmed orders per week, cancellation rate and forecast accuracy (sample history). New vs returning chart. |
| 2:20 | Close | "Next step: confirm assumptions with GoodCane, then a 4-week pilot with 20–30 WhatsApp customers." |
