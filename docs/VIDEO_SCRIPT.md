# Demo video script (about 2½ minutes)

Before recording:
- Sign in to the staff dashboard at `/admin/` in a second window.
- Record the customer site on a phone, or in the browser's mobile view at 390px wide.
- Record the staff dashboard on a laptop-sized window.

| Time | Screen | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Customer site home (`/?src=tiktok`) | "GoodCane presses fresh juice once a week. People find it on TikTok, but interest doesn't turn into a routine, and GoodCane can't see demand before production day. This turns discovery into weekly pre-orders and repeat orders." Show the next batch and the countdown to Thursday 6pm. |
| 0:15 | Flavour page, then menu | Open Passion Fruit. Add bottles from the menu; the basket bar appears. Tap **Checkout**. |
| 0:30 | Order form | Choose pickup, enter name and number. **Tick the reminder text** ("off by default, at most once a week"). Note "Pay after we confirm". **Place order**. |
| 0:50 | Receipt + phone | Order number on screen, status **Awaiting confirmation**. Show the real SMS arriving on your phone. The customer can cancel until Thursday. |
| 1:00 | Staff `/admin/` → Demand | "A separate staff app; the new order appears live. Confirmed bottles vs requests still awaiting confirmation, per flavour. The suggested batch weights pending requests by how often they've been confirmed before." |
| 1:15 | Orders | **Confirm** the new order, then **Mark paid** (payment is taken outside the app). Each change texts the customer; show the texts arriving. |
| 1:30 | SMS | Show the message log: received, confirmed, payment received. Press **Send reminder** to show the weekly reminder going to opted-in customers. |
| 1:50 | Customer → My orders | "A week later the customer comes back." The order shows **Paid**. Tap **Order this again**: it's copied into next Saturday. Place it. Two orders, two weeks. |
| 2:10 | Staff → Results | Repeat-order rate, confirmed orders per week, cancellation rate and forecast accuracy (sample history). New vs returning chart. |
| 2:25 | Close | "Next: confirm assumptions with GoodCane, then a 4-week pilot with 20–30 existing customers." |
