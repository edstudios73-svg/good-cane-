# Demo video script

Video: https://goodcane-six.vercel.app/demo.mp4 (3:01, 1080p, voice-over).

It follows the three things the brief asks to see: **ordering**, **reordering** and the **business demand view**. Everything is recorded on the live site; other customers' names and numbers are masked.

| # | Section | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro | Shop home | This is GoodCane, fresh sugarcane juice pressed in Accra. Right now most orders arrive as DMs, so every batch is a guess. We built a pre-order shop that turns interest into confirmed orders, and shows GoodCane exactly how much to press, before the cane is cut. |
| 2 | 1 · Ordering | Home: next batch, time left, bottles reserved, search, filters, favourites | Ordering. A customer taps the link from TikTok or WhatsApp, and is welcomed with Akwaaba. They see the next fresh batch, how long is left to order, and how many bottles are already reserved. They can search, filter by taste, and save favourites. |
| 3 | 1 · Ordering | Juice page, add to order | Each juice has its own page with real ingredients. Swipe between flavours, then add it to the order. |
| 4 | 1 · Ordering | Basket: plus, minus, swipe to remove | The order screen works like a phone app. Plus, minus, or swipe to remove. There is nothing to pay yet. GoodCane confirms first, so no live payments are connected. |
| 5 | 1 · Ordering | Checkout: batch, pickup, name, phone | Checkout asks for just a name and a phone number. Choose the batch, pickup or delivery, and whether to get a weekly reminder. Reminders are off by default. |
| 6 | 1 · Ordering | Receipt with order number and progress | The order is saved in a live Supabase database, and the order number goes out by SMS through SasuSync, from the GOOD CANE sender I D. The receipt shows every step, from received to collected. |
| 7 | 2 · Reordering | Receipt → "Order this again" → basket refilled | Reordering. Next week, the customer taps order this again, on the receipt or from their reminder text. The same juices go straight back into the basket. One tap, and they are ready to check out. |
| 8 | 3 · Business side | Staff sign-in | Now the business side. GoodCane staff sign in to a separate, protected dashboard. Customers' details never reach the public shop. |
| 9 | 3 · Business demand view | Demand by flavour and suggested batch | The business demand view. For each batch, GoodCane sees bottles per flavour, split into requested, confirmed and paid, with a suggested amount to press. No more guessing. |
| 10 | 3 · Orders | Confirm the new order; customer is texted | In Orders, the new order has arrived live. One tap confirms it, and the customer is texted automatically. |
| 11 | 3 · SMS | Message log and automatic texts | Every text is logged with its delivery result. Automatic texts run on their own. A ready today message on batch morning, and a nightly summary to the owner. The weekly last call reminder stays off until GoodCane approves it. |
| 12 | 3 · Results | The four pilot measures | Results tracks what the brief asked for. Repeat order rate, confirmed orders per week, cancellations, and forecast accuracy. |
| 13 | Outro | Logo, demo and code links | GoodCane. Fresh cane juice, pressed just for you, and demand you can finally plan for. Thank you. |
